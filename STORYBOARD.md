# Storyboard

The main timing is 45 seconds before the sanctuary loop begins. See `STORYBOARD_TIMELINE.md` for the second-by-second cue sheet.

| Scene | Time | Visual direction | Sound |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Overwhelm | 0–15s | Fast neon topographic video fills the mobile viewport. | Muffled static and noise start after the visitor taps. |
| Internal Agony | 15–30s | Reveal the monochrome drawing; push into the chest and agitate the circular scribbles. | Static swells with the chest movement. |
| Escape | 30–45s | Pan up the earphone wire; freeze the chest at the snap, then pull out. | Cut static and bring in the lo-fi track. |
| Sanctuary | 45s onward | Show the full art with slow contour drift, contained chest float, and smooth audio-reactive head waves. | Music continues and drives the head analyser. |

The visitor can skip to the sanctuary at any point during the cinematic. Keep the sanctuary loop calm and let the source linework remain legible.
